The AYNEHA apps

Free Android apps, with no ads, no account and no data collection. They are downloaded directly from this website.

For now the apps exist for Android only. There is no iPhone (iOS) version yet: on iPhone, use the online tools, which work in the browser.

  • AYNEHA School New

    Learn to read and write AYNEHA on your own and offline: alphabet, diacritics, syllabary, digits, finger tracing, quizzes and a transcription workshop.

  • AYNEHA Home

    The whole AYNEHA website on your phone, even offline, updated automatically when you are connected.

  • AYNEHA Keyboard

    A keyboard to write AYNEHA in all the apps on your phone, with long presses for the forms of each letter and sharing as an image.

  • AYNEHA Calc

    A calculator entirely in AYNEHA digits, read from right to left.

  • AYNEHA Horloge

    The time, day, month and Songhay year in AYNEHA, with a calendar and two home-screen widgets.

  • AYNEHA NumConvert

    A number in six scripts: AYNEHA, N'Ko, Adlam, Dogon NÈNÈ, Arabic and Western digits.

  • AYNEHA Carnet Coming soon

    A notebook entirely in AYNEHA, in preparation.

Each app has its own page with its description, download links and privacy policy (in French for now).
