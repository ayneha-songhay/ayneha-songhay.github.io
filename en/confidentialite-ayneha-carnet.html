Privacy: AYNEHA Carnet

AYNEHA Carnet is a notebook in AYNEHA, in preparation. This policy describes how it is planned to work; it will be confirmed on release.

Your notes stay with you

Notes are stored only on your device. No account, no server: they are never sent anywhere else. Uninstalling the app erases them.

Sharing

A note is only shared if you decide so, with the app you choose.

No account, no tracking

No account to create. No audience analytics, no advertising tracker, no advertising.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app changes. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with its update date.
