AYNEHA Margey Test version

Messaging without Internet: phones talk over Bluetooth and relay messages.

Download the app

Version 1.0.0 - APK file for Android

AYNEHA Margey (shown as "Margey") is a messaging app that works without Internet, without an account and without a server. Nearby phones talk over Bluetooth and form a mesh network: each phone relays the messages of the others, from one phone to the next.

It is designed for villages, markets, gatherings and every place where the connection is missing. The interface exists in three languages: AYNEHA, French and English.

Test version. The mesh network works better when many nearby phones have Margey. A few labels not yet translated are shown in French for now. Your feedback helps improve the app.

What it does

  • No Internet, no account, no phone number and no server: the app does not even have permission to access the Internet
  • Mesh network: phones talk over Bluetooth (and Wi-Fi Aware when it is on) and each phone relays the messages of the others, up to 20 hops
  • Three languages: AYNEHA, French and English. Chosen at first launch, then at any time with the globe icon at the top of each screen; in AYNEHA, the whole interface reads from right to left
  • Built-in AYNEHA keyboard, or the phone's usual keyboard, as you prefer
  • Private messages encrypted end to end: only the recipient can read them
  • Couriers: a message for someone who is away is sealed with their key and carried by other phones for 7 days, without them being able to read it
  • Nearby rooms: each room has a name and a unique 4-digit code; its messages are kept from 3 to 90 days, as chosen by its creator; 500 people at most
  • Private groups by QR code invitation: encrypted messages, 100 people at most, never deleted automatically
  • Members of groups and rooms counted with the Tata-Set method, specific to Margey
  • Photos and voice messages (60 seconds, 30 seconds in rooms)
  • Emergency alerts, written or spoken, time-stamped and signed
  • Add a contact with a QR code, share a message as an image
  • Share the app from phone to phone, without Internet

How to use it

  1. Install the app, open it and choose your language: AYNEHA, French or English.
  2. Allow Bluetooth and nearby devices. On some phones, Android also asks for location for the Bluetooth search: Margey never records or sends your position.
  3. Add a contact by scanning their QR code, or show them yours.
  4. For a nearby room, enter its name and its 4-digit code, or tap a room in the list.
  5. Keep Bluetooth on: your phone relays the messages of others and keeps the network alive.

Compatibility

Android 8 or newer. Works without Internet; Bluetooth must be on.

Settings

Language; 4-digit lock code; automatic deletion of private chats; emergency wipe; light, dark or automatic theme; text size.

Privacy

No account, no phone number, no server, no advertising. The app has no Internet access: your messages stay on the phones.
