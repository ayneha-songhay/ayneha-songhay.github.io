Start here

New to AYNEHA? Follow these five steps in order. Each one takes a few minutes, everything works in your browser, and you can come back at any time.

right to left ← start AYNEHA is written and read from right to left, letters and digits alike.

  1. 1

    Recognise the letters

    Five vowels, then twenty-two consonants. Look at each letter, say its name in your head, and spot the ones that look alike.

    See the 27 lettersPractise: Recognise
  2. 2

    Understand the diacritics

    A diacritic changes the letter: nasalised or open vowel, double letter, silent consonant. Only one diacritic per character.

    See the diacriticsPractise: Which diacritic?
  3. 3

    Read syllables

    A consonant followed by a vowel makes a syllable: the consonant is on the right, the vowel on its left.

    Practise: Read the syllableThe full syllabary in AYNEHA School
  4. 4

    Read words

    Type words you know into the transcriber, in Latin letters, and see how they are written in AYNEHA, letter by letter.

    Open the transcriberPractise: Digits
  5. 5

    Write a sentence

    Write a greeting or a wish and turn it into a card for your family and friends. On a phone, install the keyboard to write everywhere.

    Create my cardTry the keyboard

Learn offline

The AYNEHA School app covers this whole path on Android, plus tracing letters with your finger and reading texts.
