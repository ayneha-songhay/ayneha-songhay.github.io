AYNEHA Calc

A calculator entirely in AYNEHA digits.

Download the app (4.7 MB)

Version 1.3.0 - APK file for Android

AYNEHA Calc is for everyday calculations, at the market, in the shop or with friends, entirely in AYNEHA digits and from right to left.

What it does

  • Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and percentage
  • The 10 AYNEHA digits on every key, without a single Latin digit
  • Decimal results with the AYNEHA decimal comma
  • Read from right to left, like all AYNEHA writing
  • The previous calculation is shown above the result
  • Delete one digit or the whole calculation (AC)
  • Large, very readable keys

How to use it

  1. Type the digits and the operation as on an ordinary calculator.
  2. Tap « = » for the result, « AC » to clear everything.

Compatibility

Android 7 or newer. Works offline.

Privacy

No account, no server, no advertising, no analytics. Nothing you type is recorded or sent.
