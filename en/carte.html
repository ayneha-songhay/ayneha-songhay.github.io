My AYNEHA card

Write a wish, a greeting or a proverb in Songhay, the way you say it at home. It is written in AYNEHA on a card to share with your family and friends. Then submit it to AYNEHA: once reviewed, it will join the community gallery.

  1. 1

    Write your message in Songhay and choose a template.

  2. 2

    Share the card with your family and friends, on WhatsApp or elsewhere.

  3. 3

    Submit it to AYNEHA: once reviewed, it joins the gallery.

1. Choose a template

Write as you pronounce: the sound “oo” is u, “sh” is sh, “ch” as in church is c. See the landmarks

Submit my card to AYNEHA

Your card can join the gallery. It is your way of keeping the writing of your language alive: every card shared is one more word written in AYNEHA, and one more person discovering it.

Submit by WhatsApp Submit by email

The message is already filled in with your text, template and nickname. It is only sent if you send it yourself. You can attach the image, but it is not necessary.

Gallery rules

  • Every card submitted is reviewed, and corrected if necessary, before being published.
  • The nickname appears on the card exactly as you typed it.
  • A card containing an insult or disrespect towards a person is not published.
  • Every approved card is published in the gallery.
  • Published cards are under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence, like the texts of this website: anyone may share them, crediting AYNEHA and the nickname.
