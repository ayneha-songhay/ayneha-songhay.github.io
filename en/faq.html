Frequently asked questions

Discovering AYNEHA and wondering about it? Here are the answers to the most common questions. Another question: write to contact@ayneha.org.

Discover

What is AYNEHA?

An original writing system designed for Songhay by Mahamadou Issiaka MAIGA (MAIGUS), created on 2 August 2026. It has 27 basic letters (5 vowels and 22 consonants), diacritics and its own digits. See the alphabet

Is it official?

AYNEHA is an independent initiative created by MAIGUS. It is not yet recognised by an institution. It is featured on Omniglot, and the long-term goal is its encoding in the Unicode standard.

Does it replace the Latin alphabet or Ajami?

No. AYNEHA is offered as a writing system of Songhay's own, alongside the existing ones. Everyone is free to use it.

Can my children learn it at school?

Not yet within the official school system. But a child can learn it right now, alone or with family, with AYNEHA School and the website quizzes.

Why write from right to left?

It is a deliberate choice: it asserts the graphic independence of Songhay writing from the Latin alphabet. Everything is read from right to left, letters and digits alike: 2026 is written 􏽾􏽼􏽾􏾂, with the 2 of the thousands on the far right.

Which variety of Songhay does AYNEHA use?

The current reference is the Songhay of Gao. The goal is to allow all Songhay speakers to learn and write with AYNEHA. Work continues to document the differences in pronunciation and vocabulary between varieties, such as Zarma or Dendi. Learn more

Is it free?

Yes, completely. The website, online tools, font and apps are free, with no ads, no account and no data collection. The code is free software.

Writing

How do I write AYNEHA from the Latin alphabet?

With the transcriber: you type in Latin letters, the way you pronounce, and the text appears in AYNEHA. A few landmarks are enough: the sound “oo” is written u, “sh” is sh, “ch” as in church is c, a doubled letter gives the double diacritic. The transcriber applies automatic rules: it does not replace a check of each word.

Can I write AYNEHA on WhatsApp and social media?

No, not directly: only by sharing images. AYNEHA is in the Unicode Private Use Area: phones and apps do not display it yet, and text typed directly would show as squares. So send your text as an image: the keyboard and the website tools have a button for that, and an image can be read everywhere.

How do I install the font on my computer?

Download the Ayneha Pro font from the Resources page (file ayneha_pro.ttf), then:

  • Windows: right-click the file, then “Install”.
  • Mac: double-click the file, then “Install Font”.
  • Linux: double-click the file, then “Install”; or copy it into the .local/share/fonts folder of your home folder.

To type AYNEHA on a computer, use the online keyboard or the transcriber, then copy the text.

Why do I see boxes instead of letters?

The device does not have the AYNEHA font. On this website, the font loads by itself. Elsewhere (messages, documents), install the Ayneha Pro font, or send the text as an image.

The reason: AYNEHA characters are placed in Unicode's Private Use Area (PUA). They are not yet officially encoded in Unicode, so phones and computers do not know them in advance: only the Ayneha Pro font can draw them.

The apps

Are the apps available for iPhone?

Not yet: the AYNEHA apps are for Android only. On iPhone, all the website tools work in the browser: transcriber, keyboard, calculator, quiz, cards.

How do I install an app downloaded from this website?

The apps are downloaded from this website as an APK file. To install one, follow the four installation steps (in French for now).

Taking part

How can I take part in the project?

Write a card in Songhay and submit it to the gallery, tell people around you about AYNEHA, and report any mistakes you see to contact@ayneha.org.
