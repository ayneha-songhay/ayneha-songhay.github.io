Gallery rules

  • Every card submitted is reviewed, and corrected if necessary, before being published.
  • The nickname appears on the card exactly as the person typed it.
  • A card containing an insult or disrespect towards a person is not published.
  • Every approved card is published in the gallery.
  • Published cards are under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence: anyone may share them, crediting AYNEHA and the nickname.

Removing a card

You submitted a card and would like it removed? Write to contact@ayneha.org.
