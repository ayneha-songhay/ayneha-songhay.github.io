Number converter

Type a number in the system of your choice: it appears at once in AYNEHA, N'Ko, Adlam, Dogon NÈNÈ, Arabic and Western digits. Up to 17 digits.

Six ways to write a number

In AYNEHA, N'Ko, Adlam (the script of Fula) and Dogon NÈNÈ, a number is read from right to left: the first digit is on the right. In Arabic and Western digits, the digits of a number are read from left to right. The value is the same: only the digits change.

The Dogon NÈNÈ script was created by Yahya Sékou Salah GUINDO.

On your phone

The AYNEHA NumConvert app does the same conversion on Android, offline.
