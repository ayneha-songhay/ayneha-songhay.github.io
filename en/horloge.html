Clock and calendar

Your device's time, the day, the moon and the Songhay lunar calendar date, together with the Gregorian calendar, entirely in AYNEHA. Everything reads from right to left.

Reading the clock

  • At the top, the time: hours, minutes and seconds, from right to left.
  • Below, the day of the week and today's moon, then the Songhay lunar date: the month, the day and the lunar year.
  • The last line gives the Gregorian date in Songhay order: month, then day, then year. Just below, a reminder shows this reading order (month / day / year).
  • The calendar follows the Songhay lunar month: the lunar day in large type, the Gregorian day small, in orange. Under the month name, the Gregorian months it overlaps.
  • The week starts on Saturday, on the right, and ends on Friday: these two days off are shaded. The lunar day changes at sunset, where you are: by default for the main city of your time zone, or at your exact location with the Location button.
  • Holiday dots: orange for the independence days of countries with Songhay communities (flag and country name in AYNEHA), white for Mali's official holidays, green for religious holidays, purple for each day of Ramadan (it turns lighter at sunset, when the fast is broken).
  • Tap a day in the calendar to see its full date and its moon.

The buttons

  • Today (calendar): back to the current month.
  • Dial (clock): switch between digits and an analogue dial.
  • Night clock (bed): the time in full screen for the night; tap the screen to leave.
  • Share: create an image of today's date.
  • Location (pin): calculate sunset at your location. Your browser asks for permission; the location is rounded, kept on your device and never sent. The button turns orange when it is in use.
  • Adjust (moon): move the start of the lunar month one or two days forward or back, if the moon is sighted on another day.

The AYNEHA Horloge app also shows this clock on Android, with home-screen widgets.
