Clock and calendar

Your device's time, the day, the month and the Songhay year, entirely in AYNEHA, without a single Latin character. Digits are read from right to left.

Reading the clock

  • At the top, the time: hours, minutes and seconds, from right to left.
  • Below, the name of the day, the name of the month and the Songhay year.
  • The Songhay year is the Gregorian year plus 2001: 2026 + 2001 = 4027, written 􏾀􏽼􏽾􏾃.
  • The calendar starts on Sunday, on the right. Today is shown in orange.

This clock follows the Gregorian calendar. A version with the Songhay lunar calendar is planned.

On your phone

The AYNEHA Horloge app shows this clock on Android, with home-screen widgets.
