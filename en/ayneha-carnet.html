AYNEHA Carnet In preparation

A notebook entirely in AYNEHA.

Coming soon

AYNEHA Carnet is a notebook whose whole interface is written in AYNEHA, without a single Latin character. It is in preparation: it will be offered here as soon as it is ready.

What it does

  • Create, save, pin, delete and share a note
  • Search your notes, optional title
  • Lists, writing direction, AYNEHA digits
  • Offline, no account, no server

Compatibility

Android.

Privacy

No account, no server, no advertising, no analytics. Nothing you type is recorded or sent.
