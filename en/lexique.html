Songhay - AYNEHA glossary

Nearly 400 Songhay words written in AYNEHA and in Latin letters, with their meaning, sorted by theme: family, body, home, animals, numbers... The glossary comes from the creator of AYNEHA, and grows with the words you suggest.

    Suggest a word

    An everyday word, a greeting, a modern word (phone, computer, trade)? Suggest it with its meaning. Every word is reviewed before being added to the glossary.

    The form prepares a message that you send yourself, by WhatsApp or e-mail. The website records nothing.
