Teaching AYNEHA

For teachers, literacy facilitators, schools, associations and cultural clubs, in Gao and everywhere else. All materials are free and open: print them, project them, share them.

A suggested lesson

About one hour, for a first contact with the writing system.

  1. Show the poster and the reading direction: everything is read from right to left, letters and digits alike.
  2. Read and trace the 5 vowels with the first page of the tracing workbook.
  3. Add a few consonants and read syllables together.
  4. Play a quiz out loud, as a group.
  5. Everyone writes their first name in AYNEHA, then creates a card with My AYNEHA card.

An activity to share

Cards made during the lesson can be submitted to the gallery, with each learner's nickname or the group's. A nice way to show the class's work.
