Privacy / Android app

Privacy: Margey

Margey is a messaging app, usable by everyone in AYNEHA, French or English, that works without Internet: nearby phones talk over Bluetooth and relay each other's messages.

No data collected

No account, no phone number, no server, no advertising, no tracker. Messages never go through the Internet: everything stays on the phones. Margey connects to the Internet for one thing only, when you ask: checking for a new version (see "Updates").

Your identity

At first launch, your phone creates your unique code by itself, offline. Only your nickname and this code are visible to the people you show them to (QR code). You can change your nickname at any time.

Your messages

Messages are stored only on your phone and on your contact's phone. Private messages are encrypted end to end: only the recipient can read them.

Relaying (mesh network)

Every phone with Margey automatically relays the messages of others to keep the network alive. A relayed private message stays encrypted: your phone carries it without being able to read it. If you turn Bluetooth off, you become unavailable until you turn it back on.

Rooms

Messages in a room are public: every phone within range of the room can read them. They are deleted after the chosen duration, from 3 days minimum to 90 days maximum. A room holds 500 people at most.

Private groups

You can only join a group by scanning its QR invitation code: show it only to the people you invite. Group messages are encrypted: only members can read them, other phones relay them without being able to read them. A group holds 100 people at most. Its messages are never deleted by themselves; only the emergency wipe or leaving the group removes them from your phone.

Permissions

Bluetooth and nearby devices: find nearby phones, send and relay messages. Wi-Fi: direct link between compatible phones. Internet: only to check for an update on ayneha.org when you ask, and for the direct Wi-Fi link between phones. Install apps: install a downloaded update, always after you confirm. Location: required by Android on some phones for the Bluetooth search; Margey never records or sends your position. Camera: scan a QR code, only when you ask. Microphone: voice messages, only while recording. Notifications: alert you to a new message.

Sharing as an image

The image of a message is created on your phone, only for the app you choose. Margey does not keep it and does not send it anywhere else.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at once with the emergency wipe, in the Settings. You can also choose automatic deletion of private chats. Uninstalling the app removes everything it contains.

Updates

Pull the chat list down: Margey reads the latest version number on ayneha.org. If it is newer, you can download it; its fingerprint is checked, then Android asks you to confirm the installation. No personal data is sent, and nothing happens unless you pull the list.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app changes. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with its update date.
