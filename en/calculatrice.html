Calculator

Count in AYNEHA digits. The result is read from right to left. On a computer, the keyboard works too.

Reading numbers

  • The first digit of a number is on the right: read from right to left.
  • The decimal separator is the AYNEHA comma, placed at the bottom.
  • Large numbers are split into groups of three digits.
  • The calculation signs (+, −, ×, ÷, =) are the international ones.

On your phone

The AYNEHA Calc app works offline, with percentages as well.
