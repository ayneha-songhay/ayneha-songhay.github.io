AYNEHA keyboard

The same keyboard as the AYNEHA Keyboard app, right in your browser. Tap a letter to write it; keep your finger pressed on a letter marked with an orange dot to choose its double, silent, nasalised or open form.

On your phone

With the AYNEHA Keyboard app, this keyboard installs on your Android phone. Since AYNEHA is in the Unicode Private Use Area, apps such as WhatsApp do not display it yet: you write in the keyboard's test area, then share the text as an image that everyone can read.

See AYNEHA Keyboard

A four-page keyboard

The bottom keys switch from letters to symbols, to the number pad in AYNEHA digits and to emojis. Letters are arranged from right to left: the first key of each row is on the right.

Privacy of this tool: nothing you type leaves your device.
