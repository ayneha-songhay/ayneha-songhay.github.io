AYNEHA Web Editor

A word processor made for AYNEHA: write, format and export your documents as PDF or images. Lines stay in the right right-to-left order, even when they wrap. Designed for teachers, learners and researchers.

Open the editor Source code

Office software such as Word or LibreOffice scrambles AYNEHA letters as soon as a line wraps. AYNEHA Web Editor solves this: every AYNEHA line is truly written right to left, on screen, in the PDF and in the image.

What it does

  • On-screen AYNEHA keyboard: vowels, consonants, digits, and the variants of each letter (double, nasal, open, mute) in one tap.
  • Text pasted from the transcriber: it puts itself in the right order, even the “for WhatsApp / Word” copy.
  • Formatting: sizes 12 to 72, colours, highlighting, alignment, lists, verse numbers in AYNEHA digits.
  • Images: move them, resize them, place them left, centre or right, with a description in Latin or AYNEHA under each image.
  • Two-column dictionaries (AYNEHA-French, AYNEHA-English, AYNEHA-N'ko), sorted by the AYNEHA or Latin alphabetical order.
  • Several documents open at once, in tabs.
  • A4-sized page: what you see is what comes out in the PDF.
  • Your name in the footer on the left, and the site link on the right.
  • Automatic backup every 60 seconds, on your device only.
  • Full-screen reading mode, and an “Infos+” guide explaining each button.

How to use it

  1. Tap “Open the editor”.
  2. Write with the AYNEHA keyboard (keyboard button in the toolbar) or paste text from the transcriber.
  3. In the “File” menu, type your name for the footer.
  4. Export as PDF or image, or save a .ayneha file to resume later.

Install it on your phone

  1. Open the editor in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right, then “Add to Home screen”, then “Install”.
  3. The AYNEHA icon appears on your home screen: the editor opens like an app, even offline.

Compatibility

Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on phones, tablets and computers. Works offline after the first visit.

Privacy

No account, no tracker, no advertising. Your documents stay on your device.
