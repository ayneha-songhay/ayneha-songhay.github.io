Privacy / Web tool

Privacy: Transcriber

The transcriber writes in AYNEHA a Songhay text typed in Latin letters.

Everything stays in your browser

The transcription is done in your browser. Your text is not sent anywhere.

Memory of your last text

So that you find it on your next visit, your last text is kept in your browser's storage, on your device only. “Clear all” deletes it.

Copy and image

Copying goes through your device's clipboard; the image is created by your browser and saved in your downloads.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app changes. The version in force is always the one published on this page, with its update date.

Contact

For any question about this policy: contact@ayneha.org. Developed by MAIGUS.
